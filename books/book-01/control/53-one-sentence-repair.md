The scope statement above is accurate about dialogue **wording** and misleading about dialogue **paragraphing**, which was the pass's principal effect. Corrected here under `74-editorial-correction-record.md` §8.

The rule is sound where the beat and the speech share a speaker. In at least **46 places it chained across a speaker change**, producing paragraphs in which two characters speak. See `73-complete-editorial-review.md` finding D-4 for the register and reproduction method.
